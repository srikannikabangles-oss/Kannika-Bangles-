Elevated Bridal & Sangeet Ornaments for Commercial Street Shoppers

Commercial Street in Shivaji Nagar is renowned for high-fashion boutique bridal shopping, lehenga hunting, and party wear. At Sri Kannika Bangles, we provide Bangalore brides and party-goers with handcrafted designer jewellery: from luminous Polki Kundan chokers to sparkling CZ diamond bangles and traditional antique temple harams. Every piece is sculpted to withstand humidity, sweat, and long wedding receptions without tarnishing.
