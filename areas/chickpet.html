Why Bangalore Brides Choose Sri Kannika Over Crowded Chickpet Lanes

Chickpet is legendary in Bangalore as a massive wholesale trading hub for wedding sarees and imitation jewellery. However, navigating through packed alleyways, uncertain base metal qualities, crowded counters, and no-trial return policies can turn bridal shopping into a stressful ordeal.

Sri Kannika Bangles offers the perfect balance: wholesale-transparent pricing combined with boutique Malleshwaram craftsmanship. For over three decades (since 1991), our master artisans have handcrafted temple harams, uncut Kundan chokers, and bespoke bridal kada stacks with 24K micro-gold plating and skin-safe alloys.

Whether you live in Chickpet, BVK Iyengar Road, Avenue Road, or Majestic, you can browse our complete collection online, send your saree photo for custom WhatsApp matching, or receive insured express delivery within 24 to 48 hours.
