How to Choose the Perfect Bangle Sizes for Bangalore Brides

Getting the right wrist measurement is essential when buying online from our jewelry catalog. A properly fitted bangle slides smoothly over the hand without causing discomfort, while remaining secure on the wrist. To help our buyers across the city, we recommend this standard measurement process developed by our master craftsmen:

1. Inner Diameter Measurement: Measure the inside diameter of a rigid bangle that currently fits you comfortably. Measure across the widest internal center point in millimeters. Standard Indian bangle sizes correspond directly to inner diameters: Size 2.4 (57.2mm), Size 2.6 (60.3mm), Size 2.8 (63.5mm), and Size 2.10 (66.7mm).

2. Hand Circumference Technique: Bring your thumb and little finger together across your palm as if putting on a bangle. Measure around the widest part of your knuckles with a flexible tape, then match the measurement to our size conversion table.

3. Adjustable Openable Kadas: If you are selecting a gift or prefer effortless wear, our selection includes openable screw kadas that unclip smoothly from the side, accommodating varying hand widths easily without requiring precise knuckle measurements.

Our customer support team is always available to guide buyers through sizing questions over phone or WhatsApp, ensuring complete confidence before placing your order.

Jewelry Care & Maintenance — Preserving Long-Lasting Shimmer

Every creation from Sri Kannika Bangles is designed to remain a prized part of your jewelry box. To ensure your gold-plated, Kundan, and CZ stone pieces stay radiant for years, follow these practical care guidelines recommended by our master artisans:

Apply Cosmetics First: Always finish applying perfumes, body lotions, hairsprays, and makeup before wearing your bangles. Chemical compounds and alcohol ingredients in cosmetics can react with micro-gold finishes over time and dull stone clarity.

Airtight Storage: Store each bangle set separately in dry zip-lock pouches or soft cotton-lined boxes. Keeping jewelry away from direct humidity prevents natural oxidation and maintains stone brilliance across seasons.

Avoid Direct Moisture: Do not submerge Kundan or micro-plated bangles in water or chemical cleansers. Dust off delicate filigree work gently with a dry microfiber cloth to keep your pieces pristine.
