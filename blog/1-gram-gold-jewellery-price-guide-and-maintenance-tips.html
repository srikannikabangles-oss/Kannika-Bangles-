1 Gram Gold Jewellery Price & Care Guide

Everything brides and jewellery lovers need to know about micro gold plating, pricing, and 10-year maintenance

Home Blog 1 Gram Gold Guide

With 24K gold rates continuously reaching new historic peaks, modern Indian brides and festival shoppers are increasingly turning to 1 gram micro gold plated jewellery. These mastercrafted ornaments deliver the regal grandeur, intricate Nakshi craftsmanship, and substantial weight of solid temple gold at a sensible fraction of the price.

However, many buyers wonder: What exactly is 1 gram gold? How does its pricing work? And how do you ensure the golden shine doesn't fade over time? In this guide, our master jewellers at Sri Kannika Bangles break down the metallurgical process, transparent pricing comparisons, and 5 essential maintenance rules.

💎 What "1 Gram Gold" Really Means: In genuine jewellery manufacturing, 1 gram gold plating refers to high-precision electro-chemical deposition where genuine 24K gold is bonded onto a high-density brass or copper alloy core, then protected with a micro-sealant. The result is indistinguishable from solid hallmarked gold to the naked eye.

5 Master Care Rules to Keep 1 Gram Gold Shining for 10+ Years

Follow these simple golden rules to protect your micro-plating from oxidation, sweat discoloration, and accidental abrasion:

1. The "Last On, First Off" Rule (Perfume & Hairspray Protection)

Chemicals such as alcohol in perfumes, hairsprays, body lotions, and makeup fixers are the #1 cause of plating degradation. Always put your jewellery on after your makeup and fragrances have completely dried. When returning from a wedding, take your jewellery off before using makeup removers.

2. Never Store in Velvet Boxes (Use Airtight Zip-Locks)

Many traditional jewellery boxes use industrial glues inside the velvet lining that release sulfur gases, causing rapid tarnishing. Always store each piece individually in an airtight zip-lock plastic pouch with a small silica gel packet.

3. Wipe Away Sweat & Body Oils After Every Wear

Before putting your ornaments back into their pouches, gently buff them with a dry, clean microfiber cloth (such as an optical lens cloth). This removes body moisture and perspiration salts that would otherwise slowly erode the protective lacquer.

4. Keep Completely Dry (No Water or Harsh Cleaners)

Never wear your 1 gram gold jewellery while bathing, washing hands, or swimming. Avoid using liquid chemical cleaners, soaps, or ultrasonic jewelry cleaners, as these can strip the micro gold layer.

5. Separate Storing to Prevent Stone Scratches

Store heavy kadas, delicate pearl chokers, and faceted AD stone necklaces in separate compartments to avoid metal-on-metal rubbing during travel.
