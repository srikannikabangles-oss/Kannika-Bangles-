Top Places for Bridal Imitation Jewellery Shopping in Bangalore

Bangalore Wedding Shopping Guide • Published September 2026 • 7 Min Read

Planning a wedding in Bangalore is an exhilarating experience. With countless heritage markets and modern jewelry hubs spread across the Garden City, brides often wonder: Where should I shop for high-quality, authentic-looking bridal imitation jewellery that won't compromise on craftsmanship or comfort? Here is the definitive local guide.

1. The 4 Major Jewellery Shopping Hubs in Bangalore

A. Malleshwaram (Sampige Road & 8th-9th Cross) — Heritage & Trust

Malleshwaram is celebrated as the cultural soul of North and West Bangalore. If you are seeking traditional South Indian bridal jewelry (antique matte gold finish, Goddess Lakshmi harams, Kemp stone bangles, and bespoke size matching), Malleshwaram is unmatched.

Showrooms like Sri Kannika Bangles (serving brides since 1991 on 9th Cross, East Park Road) specialize in micro-gold plated bridal suites that have the weight, luster, and hand-chiseled perfection of solid heirloom gold.

B. Chickpet & Balepet — Wholesale & Bulk Shopping

Chickpet is Bangalore's historic commercial trading nerve center. It is ideal if you are buying bulk wedding favors, return gifts, or simple glass bangles in large cartons. However, the narrow congested lanes, lack of dedicated trial rooms, and mass-produced finishes make bridal suite coordination challenging for brides seeking bespoke personalized consultations.

C. Jayanagar (3rd & 4th Block) — South Bangalore Retail

Jayanagar’s shopping complexes host multiple multi-brand imitation stores and chain outlets. It offers convenient ready-made options for South Bangalore residents, though pricing is often higher due to prime retail overheads.

D. Commercial Street & Shivajinagar — North Indian & Indo-Western

If your wedding events include a vibrant Sangeet or Bollywood-themed cocktail party, Commercial Street is famous for uncut Kundan, mirror work, and pastel meenakari sets imported from Jaipur and Mumbai.

3. 4 Quality Checkpoints Before Buying Bridal Imitation Jewellery

  • 1. Plating Quality & Micron Depth: Always choose 24K micro gold plated brass rather than cheap flash plating. Flash plating tarnishes within a single humid evening, while micro gold plating retains its heirloom sheen for years.
  • 2. Stone Setting (Prongs vs. Glue): Look closely at the Kemp rubies and Kundan stones. High-grade bridal pieces use miniature prong claw settings that keep gems securely locked during energetic dance celebrations.
  • 3. Weight & Comfort Balance: Heavy necklaces can cause neck stiffness after 6 hours of ceremonies. Premium handcrafted sets feature hollow-embossed backings that look grand and regal while remaining surprisingly lightweight.
  • 4. Non-Allergenic Core: Ensure the base metal is hypoallergenic copper or brass alloy, completely lead and nickel-free to prevent skin irritation under hot stage spotlights.
