2. Antique Temple Haram

3. Royal Bridal Choker

A snug, high-neck choker adorned with uncut stones, freshwater pearl drops, and rich kemp rubies designed to frame the collarbone.

Traditional inverted V-shaped bridal armlet featuring peacock and Goddess motifs, worn snugly above the elbow on silk blouse sleeves.
