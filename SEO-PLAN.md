# Kannika Bangles SEO Plan

## Current technical status

- Primary categories: `/bangles` and `/pendant-sets`.
- Product URLs: `/product/1` through `/product/28`.
- Clean canonical URLs are used for the public routes.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are present and the sitemap now includes all 28 products.
- A square 64px PNG, 180px Apple icon, and SVG favicon are included in every HTML page.
- `/login`, `/cart`, `/checkout`, and `/wishlist` should remain `noindex`; they are customer utility pages, not search landing pages.
- There is currently no admin panel. The project has no admin route, admin UI, product CRUD screen, role-protected dashboard, or admin API. `seed.js` is the current catalogue-management mechanism.

## Page-by-page targets

| Page group | Primary search intent | SEO action |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | bridal jewellery, bridal bangles Bangalore | Homepage title, description, LocalBusiness/Organization/WebSite schema, strong internal links |
| `/bangles` | bridal bangles Bangalore, Kundan bangles | CollectionPage + ItemList + unique category copy |
| `/pendant-sets` | pendant sets Bangalore, bridal pendant jewellery | CollectionPage + ItemList + unique category copy |
| `/shop` | Indian wedding jewellery Bangalore | Broad collection landing page; link prominently to the two categories |
| `/product/:id` | individual product searches | Unique title, description, image, Product/Offer schema, canonical URL |
| `/about` | jewellery shop trust and heritage | Business story, founder/heritage proof, LocalBusiness signals |
| `/contact` | jewellery showroom Bangalore | Address, phone, hours, map, contact details and LocalBusiness consistency |
| `/areas/*` | jewellery near Bangalore locality | Keep only useful, genuinely local pages; add unique local proof and store directions |
| `/blog/*` | informational bridal jewellery searches | Publish helpful, original guides and link to relevant collections/products |
| policy pages | delivery, exchange, returns | Keep crawlable for trust; link from product and checkout journeys |

## Recommended new pages

1. `/bridal-jewellery-bangalore` — the main commercial landing page for bridal jewellery beyond the two product categories.
2. `/kundan-jewellery-bangalore` — Kundan intent page with real product examples and care guidance.
3. `/jadau-jewellery-bangalore` — only if the store actually sells Jadau products.
4. `/wedding-jewellery-combos` — curated bangle + pendant-set combinations with clear styling advice.
5. `/bangle-size-guide` — useful pre-purchase guide with a size chart and measurement instructions.
6. `/jewellery-care-guide` — care, storage, cleaning, plating, and stone-safety guidance.
7. `/shipping-and-delivery-bangalore` — delivery areas, timelines, fees, and order expectations.
8. `/reviews` or a verified testimonials section — only with authentic customer reviews and permission.

Do not create thin pages for every keyword or locality. Each new page should have original content, real imagery, clear internal links, and a distinct customer need.

## 90-day execution plan

### Days 1–14: technical and trust foundation

- Verify the live domain in Google Search Console and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Request indexing for the homepage, both categories, contact page, and strongest products.
- Connect and verify Google Business Profile; keep name, address, phone, hours, and website identical everywhere.
- Run PageSpeed Insights and address Core Web Vitals, especially image weight, font loading, and layout shift.
- Add Merchant Center product data once prices, stock, shipping, and return information are final.

### Days 15–45: commercial landing pages

- Build the bridal jewellery, Kundan, and wedding-combos pages only where inventory supports them.
- Add unique category introductions, FAQs, breadcrumbs, and strong internal links.
- Improve every product with accurate material, size, availability, shipping, return, and image information.

### Days 46–90: authority and local growth

- Publish two genuinely useful bridal-jewellery guides per month.
- Earn local links from wedding planners, bridal boutiques, Bangalore directories, and relevant publications.
- Collect authentic reviews and answer Google Business Profile questions.
- Review Search Console queries monthly and improve pages that receive impressions but few clicks.

## Measurement

Track indexed pages, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, branded vs non-branded queries, product rich-result eligibility, organic product views, WhatsApp enquiries, add-to-cart rate, and completed orders.
