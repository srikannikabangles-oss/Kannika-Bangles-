A Legacy Sculpted in Tradition

For over two decades, the House of Kannika has stood as a guardian of heritage metalcraft, weaving the romance of Indian royalty into timeless jewellery caskets.

Originating from the historic cultural heart of Malleshwaram, Bangalore, every curation is meticulously handcrafted by master artisans—blending ancestral heritage with a sophisticated modern aesthetic.

Our flagship store on Sampige Road has been a beloved destination for generations of families. We specialize in handcrafted bridal bangles, wedding sets, majestic chokers, harams, and designer earrings that add a touch of grace to your most cherished moments.
