Our Heritage

Crafting Beauty Since Generations

Nestled in the heart of Sampige Road, Malleshwaram, Bangalore, Kannika Bangles has been a trusted name in handcrafted bangles for over two decades. What started as a humble passion for timeless adornments has blossomed into a beloved destination for brides, fashion enthusiasts, and lovers of fine craftsmanship alike.

At Kannika Bangles, every bangle is more than just an accessory — it's a piece of art crafted with care. From elegant, budget-friendly designs perfect for everyday wear to luxurious bridal collections that make your special day unforgettable, we curate pieces that blend tradition with contemporary style.

Our skilled team of artisans meticulously assembles and styles each bangle set to perfection, ensuring that every piece that leaves our store carries the warmth of handcrafted love and the sparkle of unmatched quality. Whether you're looking for AD stone bangles, micro gold-plated sets, or intricate Jadau jewellery, we have something special waiting for you.
