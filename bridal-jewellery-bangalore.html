Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I buy the best bridal imitation jewellery in Bangalore?

Sri Kannika Bangles on Sampige Road, Malleshwaram is one of Bangalore's most trusted showrooms for handcrafted bridal jewellery, offering antique matte gold necklace sets, Kundan chokers, temple harams, and bridal kada stacks since 1991.

What type of polish is used on your bridal jewellery sets?

All our jewellery pieces feature premium 24K micro gold plating with an anti-tarnish protective coat, providing the authentic radiant luster of real heirloom gold while being skin-friendly and hypoallergenic.

Do you deliver bridal jewellery across Bangalore and India?

Yes! We offer 24–48 hour express courier dispatch across all Bangalore localities (Malleshwaram, Indiranagar, Jayanagar, Koramangala, Whitefield, Rajajinagar) and 10-day insured delivery across India.

Can I get custom bridal bangle stacks curated for my wedding saree?

Yes. You can send photos of your wedding saree or lehenga via WhatsApp (+91 98447 58450) or visit our Malleshwaram showroom for bespoke bridal stack curation matching your exact wrist size and color palette.
