How We Deliver

Kannika Bangles uses a careful, personalised delivery approach to ensure your jewellery arrives safely:

  • Within Bangalore — We use our own trusted delivery team for door-to-door delivery, ensuring your jewellery is handled with the care it deserves
  • Outside Bangalore — We partner with reputed courier services (e.g., Delhivery, Blue Dart) with full insurance and tracking
  • All items are packed in tamper-proof, gift-ready packaging to ensure safe transit
  • You will receive a WhatsApp notification once your order is out for delivery

Delayed or Failed Delivery

While we strive to deliver within the promised timeframe, delays may occur due to:

  • Public holidays or festivals
  • Adverse weather or natural events
  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery address
  • Customer unavailability at the time of delivery

If your order does not arrive within the promised window, please contact us at +91 98447 58450 and we will investigate and resolve the issue promptly.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive:

  • A WhatsApp message confirming your order is on its way
  • For courier shipments — a tracking number to monitor your package
  • Our delivery team will call you 30 minutes before arrival for Bangalore deliveries

Make sure your phone number is correct and reachable at the time of delivery. Re-delivery attempts may not be possible for refused or missed deliveries.
