How We Deliver

Kannika Bangles uses a careful, personalised delivery approach to ensure your jewellery arrives safely:

  • Within Bangalore — We use our own trusted delivery team for door-to-door delivery, ensuring your jewellery is handled with the care it deserves
  • Outside Bangalore — We partner with reputed courier services (e.g., Delhivery, Blue Dart) with full insurance and tracking
  • All items are packed in tamper-proof, gift-ready packaging to ensure safe transit
  • You will receive a WhatsApp notification once your order is out for delivery

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive:

  • A WhatsApp message confirming your order is on its way
