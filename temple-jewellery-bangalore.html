Temple Jewellery FAQs

What makes temple jewellery unique for South Indian weddings?

Temple jewellery originated in the Chola and Pandya dynasties to adorn deities. It features intricate hand-carved Nakshi motifs of Goddess Lakshmi, peacocks, elephants, and Kemp spinels, symbolizing prosperity and divine grace on the wedding day.

Does antique temple jewellery look like real gold in wedding photography?

Yes. Our temple jewellery collection uses authentic matte reddish-gold micro plating that diffuses flash reflection, creating the rich, warm, heirloom radiance of traditional temple gold in 4K photography and video.

Is your temple jewellery suitable for classical dance (Bharatanatyam / Kuchipudi)?

Absolutely. Dancers and brides alike appreciate our durable brass-alloy base with lightweight hollow-embossed backings, preventing ear-lobe stretching or neck fatigue during long performances or ceremonies.
